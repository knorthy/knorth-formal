# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring teams. They arrive from a résumé or profile link, scan quickly, and decide whether Tiffany is worth an interview.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Tiffany Lyssa, developer. It shows her projects, skills, education and credentials, writing, and GitHub activity. Success is a recruiter understanding who she is and what she has built within one visit, and following a link to contact her or to her work.

## Operating Context

One page, read once and quickly, usually on a desktop during screening and sometimes on a phone from a link.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections confirmed by the owner: projects, skills, education and credentials, writing, GitHub activity.
- Existing codebase: Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, Tailwind CSS 4, TypeScript.
- Undecided: exact job title, bio, project list, links, school, credentials, blog posts, GitHub username.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Tiffany Lyssa.
- The owner made these binding: monochrome, minimalist.
- Reference the owner named: https://immanbufi.dev/ (sets the craft bar and the section set).

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. The owner chose clearly marked placeholders for bio, projects, skills, education, credentials, writing, and GitHub activity. Every placeholder must read as a sample and be listed for replacement. Do not present invented employers, awards, certificates, or contribution counts as real.

## Product Principles

1. The work leads; the interface stays out of its way.
2. A recruiter should get the answer in one scan.
3. Nothing invented is passed off as fact.
4. One project gets attention at a time.
